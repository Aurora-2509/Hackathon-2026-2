What should the catalog contain?

Required: id, name, category. Categories: shirt, pants, shoes, dresses, outerwear, bags, accessories, other. Optional: color, style, image_url, product_url, price, currency. Unknown colors and styles stay unknown. Original category labels are retained. Import a JSON or CSV item export; raw Parquet files need conversion first. JSON accepts an array or {"items": [...]}. Prices must be nonnegative numbers. CSV supports quoted fields.

THE POLYVORE CLOSET

Everything, in its place.

Source: mvasil/polyvore-outfits. Polyvore access requires approval. The closet also includes dress, shirt, and pants photos from Clothing Dataset Small.

AI model: trained on the xthan/polyvore-dataset text catalog (Han et al., 2017). Closet dataset: Vasileva et al., Learning Type-Aware Embeddings for Fashion Compatibility, ECCV 2018. CC BY 4.0. Additional photos: Clothing Dataset Small, Alexey Grigorev and contributors (repository license retained). Categories are grouped for browsing; color and style metadata are not invented.
